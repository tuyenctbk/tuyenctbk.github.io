QuakeCast Privacy Policy

100% Ad-Free · Zero User Tracking · Open Telemetry

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction & Core Commitment

QuakeCast ("we", "our", or "the app") is an independent seismic monitoring and earthquake awareness application designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. We believe that life-safety and scientific earthquake telemetry must never be coupled with invasive user tracking, behavioral profiling, or advertising networks.

QuakeCast collects zero personal data. We do not maintain user accounts, do not operate tracking servers, and do not sell or share any user telemetry with third parties.

2. Information Handling & Device Permissions

QuakeCast requires minimal device permissions to perform its core scientific and safety functions:

Geographic Location Coordinates

How it is used: If configured by the user, geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) are utilized exclusively on the local device to calculate the distance and bearing to earthquake epicenters, determine whether a tremor is within the user's custom warning radius, and articulate local spoken warnings via on-device Text-to-Speech (TTS).

Storage & Transmission: Location coordinates are stored strictly inside private, local device storage (Android Jetpack DataStore / Room). Your location is never transmitted to our servers, USGS servers, or any third party.

Public USGS Seismic Feeds (Network Access)

QuakeCast makes direct HTTPS requests to public United States Geological Survey (USGS) GeoJSON feeds (earthquake.usgs.gov). These requests retrieve publicly available earthquake data (magnitude, coordinates, depth, timestamp, and tsunami warnings). No user identifiers, device serial numbers, or advertising IDs are attached to these network calls.

Physical Motion Sensor (Shake Detection)

The app accesses the device's hardware accelerometer via Android's SensorManager strictly when the "Shake to Refresh" feature is enabled. The physical motion data is analyzed instantaneously in volatile memory on-device to detect a physical tremor or shake gesture for manual feed refreshing. No sensor logs are permanently stored or transmitted.

Local On-Device Storage (Room & DataStore)

We store data locally on your device using Android Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore Preferences for the following purposes:

  • Caching USGS earthquake GeoJSON records so the 3D globe and event lists remain functional when the device is offline or in emergency conditions.
  • Saving your preferred proximity alert radius, reference location name, and coordinates.
  • Saving user preferences including theme mode (Dark, Light, System) and shake sensitivity thresholds.
  • Tracking local app lifecycle telemetry (such as successful feed refreshes) to ensure engagement prompts (updates, ratings, sharing) observe strict 30-day minimum cooldowns and emergency safety lockouts.

3. Third-Party Services & Voluntary Billing

No Third-Party Advertising or Analytics: QuakeCast contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics), and no third-party telemetry tools.

Voluntary Developer Contributions: Users may voluntarily support the independent development of QuakeCast through Google Play In-App Billing, PayPal, or Ko-fi. When making a contribution via Google Play, payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play Services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. QuakeCast never sees, collects, or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

QuakeCast does not solicit, collect, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Data Retention & User Control

Because all user preferences, cached earthquake records, and settings reside strictly on your local device, you retain full control over your data at all times. You can delete all cached data and preferences instantly by selecting "Clear Data" or "Uninstall" in your Android system settings for QuakeCast.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new Android OS capabilities or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document and will continue to uphold our core zero-tracking philosophy.
