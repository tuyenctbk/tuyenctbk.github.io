Privacy Policy

App Name: SwAIft

Package Name: com.soloprono.swaift

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

Summary: SwAIft is designed with a privacy-first architecture. Your automation flows, device trigger data, and API keys remain on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SwAIft ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and device information processed through our application. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the SwAIft mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Use

SwAIft is a local-first automation tool. We do not maintain external servers to harvest, track, or profile user data.

A. User-Provided API Keys (Bring Your Own Key - BYOK)

To use AI-powered features (such as the Zero-Logic AI Routine Studio), you may provide a Google Gemini API Key. This key is stored securely on your device's local storage and is transmitted directly and exclusively to Google's Gemini API servers to process your prompts.

B. Device Permissions & Sensor Data

To execute automated background routines, SwAIft may request access to specific Android permissions based on the features you choose to enable:

  • Location Access (Fine/Coarse): Used strictly for location-based geofencing triggers (e.g., executing routines when arriving at work or leaving home).
  • Bluetooth & Wi-Fi State: Used to detect network connectivity triggers (e.g., connecting to car audio or home Wi-Fi).
  • Activity Recognition: Used to detect motion triggers (e.g., walking, driving).
  • Do Not Disturb / Notification Policy: Used to automate sound and focus profiles based on your configured routines.
  • Post Notifications: Used to display foreground service status and execution logs.

All sensor evaluation and trigger checks are performed locally on your device.

C. Local Data Storage

SwAIft utilizes a local Room database to store your configured automation flows, pre-built templates, and execution history (Transparency Audit Log). This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it.

3. Third-Party Services

When using AI generation features, SwAIft communicates with third-party APIs as directed by your configuration:

  • Google Gemini API: Prompts entered into the AI Routine Studio or Q&A Assistant are sent directly to Google's Gemini API endpoints. The processing of these prompts is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Because all automation rules, execution logs, and API credentials are kept in sandboxed local app storage, your data is protected by Android's built-in security model. We do not operate remote databases that could be subject to data breaches.

5. Your Choices & Data Control

  • Permission Control: You can grant or revoke system permissions at any time through your device's Settings > Apps > SwAIft > Permissions.
  • Data Deletion: Clearing the app's cache and storage, or uninstalling SwAIft, immediately and permanently deletes all stored routines, logs, and API keys from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

SwAIft is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
