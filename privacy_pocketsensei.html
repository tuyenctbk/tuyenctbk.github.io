Privacy Policy for Pocket Sensei

Effective Date: November 12, 2026 • Developer Contact: tuyenctbk@gmail.com

Pocket Sensei ("the App") is an interactive 3D language, pronunciation, and voice roleplay coach. We respect your privacy and keep the App completely free of third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, and data brokers. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use Pocket Sensei, how on-device and optional cloud modes work, and how your data is protected.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in with Google via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication, we receive your Firebase User ID, display name, and email address strictly to authenticate your account and sync your learning progress.
  • Learning & Cloud Sync Data (Cloud Firestore): Your practiced vocabulary words, translations, phonetic breakdowns, mastery levels (1–5), daily streaks, practice minutes, roleplay session summaries, and Dojo Accent League leaderboard stats are stored securely in Cloud Firestore under your authenticated account.
  • On-Device Local Storage (Room Database & SharedPreferences):
    • Vocabulary words and cached pronunciation evaluations are stored locally in an encrypted-at-rest OS sandbox Room database so repeated drills load immediately offline with zero cloud usage.
    • App interaction events (such as completed speaking milestones, screen navigation, and prompt cooldown timestamps) are stored strictly on-device to enforce non-intrusive prompt safeguards and prevent repetitive dialogs.
    • AI coaching mode selection, daily cloud-call counters, and supporter status flags are stored locally in private SharedPreferences.
  • Personal Gemini API Key (Optional BYOK Mode): If you choose to enter your own Gemini API key in Profile settings ("Bring Your Own Key"), that key is saved strictly in private on-device SharedPreferences (Context.MODE_PRIVATE). It is never transmitted to our servers, Firebase, or any third party other than Google's Gemini API endpoint when your device makes a direct BYOK evaluation request. You may edit or clear your key at any time.
  • Microphone, Real-Time Waveform & Speech Audio: Pocket Sensei requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission only when you tap the microphone to speak in Accent Coach, Voice Roleplay, or Dojo Speak Blitz. Audio energy and pitch contour metrics are processed locally on-device in memory to render the real-time waveform visualizer and pitch comparison curve; raw microphone audio files are never uploaded or stored by Pocket Sensei.
  • AI & Offline Pronunciation Processing: Depending on your chosen coaching engine:
    • Offline ($0) & Dojo Speak Blitz: Speech transcripts and pitch metrics are evaluated 100% locally on your device with zero network transmission.
    • Hybrid Mode: Repeated phrases resolve from your local Room cache first; new evaluations within the daily allowance use Firebase AI Logic (Gemini) and seamlessly fall back to on-device scoring if offline or capped.
    • Your Gemini Key (BYOK): Spoken transcripts and target phrases are sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API using your personal key.
  • On-Device 3D Rendering: The interactive 3D Sensei avatar runs locally inside an isolated WebView from bundled app assets with no external telemetry scripts.
  • Voluntary Purchases & Donations: Optional supporter tiers processed through Google Play Billing are verified via Google Play purchase tokens and acknowledged locally to unlock the permanent Active Supporter badge and disable donation prompts. External links to PayPal (paypal.me / live QR code) or Ko-fi open outside the App and are governed by those services' respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • Render real-time audio waveforms, pitch accent contours, phonetic tips, and interactive 3D avatar lip-sync animations.
  • Evaluate pronunciation accuracy in Offline, Hybrid, or user-configured BYOK modes.
  • Synchronize your vocabulary bank, mastery progress, streaks, and league scores across signed-in sessions.
  • Honor Google Play supporter entitlements and apply prompt cooldown safeguards so rate, share, update, and support prompts never interrupt on startup.
  • Protect cloud API endpoints from abuse using Firebase App Check.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we never display third-party ads. Data is processed only by essential infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Firebase AI Logic): Account authentication, cloud progress sync, app integrity checks, and Hybrid cloud language evaluation.
  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: Google Sign-In credentials and optional in-app supporter transactions.
  • Google Gemini API (only when BYOK is enabled by you): Direct client-side evaluation requests using your own API key.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your cloud learning data remains stored while your account is active. Local Room cache (vocabulary words, cached evaluations, telemetry logs), preferences, and any saved BYOK API key can be cleared immediately at any time via your Android system App Storage settings, the Clear Key button in Profile, or by uninstalling the App. To request permanent deletion of your Cloud Firestore account records, email tuyenctbk@gmail.com from your registered Google account email.

5. Children's Privacy

Pocket Sensei is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data rights, contact us at tuyenctbk@gmail.com.
