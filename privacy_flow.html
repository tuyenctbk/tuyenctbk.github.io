Privacy Policy for FLOW

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Thank you for using FLOW. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

FLOW is designed with privacy at its core. We prioritize local processing and storage:

  • Local Data Storage: Your focus sessions, session histories, preferences, and reflections are stored locally on your device using a local database (Room). This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.
  • Audio Focus and Playback: The app utilizes system audio focus and generates ambient soundscapes locally on your device. We do not record, transmit, or store any microphone or audio data.

2. How We Use Information

Since all focus and session metrics are stored locally on your device, we use this information solely to:

  • Display your personal focus history and statistics within the app.
  • Provide continuous, personalized mindfulness and breathing experiences.

3. Third-Party Services

FLOW does not integrate intrusive third-party trackers or ad networks. Essential platform services (such as Android OS APIs) operate in accordance with their respective platform privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Because your data is stored securely on your device, you maintain full control over your personal session records.

5. Children's Privacy

FLOW is suitable for users of all ages seeking mindfulness and focus tools. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@soloprono.flow.
