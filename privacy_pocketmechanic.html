Privacy Policy

Application: PocketMechanic (com.soloprono.pocketmechanic)
Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Contact: tuyenctbk@gmail.com

PocketMechanic ("we", "our", or "the app") is an ad-free DIY mechanical, automotive, plumbing, and home appliance diagnostic tool. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum data strictly required to operate the features you actively choose to use.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • Account & Sync Information (Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication): When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic account identifier (Firebase UID), display name, and email address strictly to authenticate your session and isolate your personal Garage equipment and cloud-synced Repair Logs in Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • User-Submitted Diagnostic Inputs (Audio, Photos, Text & Voice): When you tap Analyze or use Hands-Free Voice Mode, the audio clip, photo, equipment model, or symptom description you explicitly capture or enter is transmitted securely over HTTPS to Google's Gemini AI service (via Firebase AI Logic or, if configured by you, your own personal Bring-Your-Own-Key Gemini API key) solely to generate your mechanical diagnosis and repair steps. We never record audio or capture camera frames in the background.
  • On-Device Diagnosis History & Preferences (Room & DataStore): Your diagnosis history, local search index, flashlight/UI preferences, and personal BYOK Gemini API key (if entered) are stored privately on your device inside Android's sandboxed app storage. Your personal BYOK API key is never uploaded to our servers or logged to Firestore.
  • Local App Event Telemetry: To avoid interrupting you with repetitive update, rating, or share prompts, PocketMechanic stores lightweight activity counters and timestamps locally on your device (such as launch counts, completed diagnoses, and prompt dismissal cooldowns). You can inspect this local event log directly inside the Garage tab.

2. Device Permissions Explained

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Requested only when you record an engine/appliance/plumbing sound clip or start Hands-Free Voice Mode.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Requested only when you open the live part/error-code viewfinder or control the hardware LED inspection flashlight. Selecting an existing photo uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker.
  • Internet & Network State: Required for Google Sign-In, cloud Firestore sync, and online AI diagnostics. Offline Quick-Reference diagnostics work without internet access.
  • No Location Permission Required: The "Find Parts Nearby" and "Nearby Repair Shop" actions launch your installed Google Maps app via standard map search intents without requesting GPS or location permissions inside PocketMechanic.

3. Third-Party Services & Payments

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Firebase AI Logic): Provides secure sign-in, per-user cloud sync, abuse protection, and keyless Gemini diagnostic processing. See Firebase Privacy & Security.
  • Google AI Studio / Gemini API (Optional BYOK Mode): If you choose to supply your own Gemini API key, requests are sent directly from your device to Google's Generative Language API under your own Google AI Studio account terms.
  • Google Play Billing, In-App Review & In-App Updates: Voluntary supporter contributions are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store credit card numbers or banking details. Optional external links to PayPal or Ko-fi open in your browser or respective app under their own privacy policies.
  • No Advertising Networks or Data Brokers: PocketMechanic contains zero third-party ad SDKs and never sells, rents, or trades your personal data.

4. Data Security & Retention

All cloud traffic uses TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit. Cloud Firestore records are protected by strict per-user security rules so only your authenticated account can read or write your Garage and Repair Log entries. Local diagnosis history remains on your device until you delete entries, clear app data, or uninstall PocketMechanic.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

  • Delete Individual Records: You may delete any Garage equipment entry or Repair Log diagnosis directly inside the app at any time.
  • Remove Personal API Key: Open AI Engine & BYOK and tap Clear to erase your stored key immediately from device preferences.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion Request: To request full deletion of your cloud-synced account records, email tuyenctbk@gmail.com from your registered Google account email with the subject "PocketMechanic Data Deletion Request". We process deletion requests within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

PocketMechanic is designed for adult DIYers, vehicle owners, and homeowners working with mechanical, electrical, and plumbing equipment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this page and inside the app.
