Official Privacy Policy

ChibiPet: 3D Habit Companion

Effective Date: November 14, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 14, 2026  |  Package ID: com.aistudio.chibipet.mzxqkp

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to ChibiPet ("the App"), a 3D virtual pet companion and daily habit-building application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and applicable global privacy laws.

Key Privacy Summary:
  • We never sell your personal data or share it with third-party advertising networks.
  • You can use ChibiPet in Guest Explorer Mode without signing in, or sign in with Google to back up and sync your pet across devices.
  • Habit proof photos captured via the camera or selected from the Android Photo Picker are used strictly for on-demand habit verification and are not stored permanently on external servers.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

2.1 Account & Authentication Information (Optional)

When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (via Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), we collect and store:

  • Firebase User Identifier (UID): Used to associate your cloud-synced pet profile, habit quests, chat memory, and daily AI quota with your account.
  • Display Name, Email Address, and Profile Photo URL: Displayed in your in-app Account & Cloud Sync panel and used to personalize your pet owner profile.

If you select Guest Explorer Mode, no Google account credentials are required or collected.

2.2 App Activity, Pet Progress & User-Generated Content

To provide core gameplay and habit tracking, the App stores the following user-authored records (in Google Cloud Firestore when signed in, or locally on your device in Guest Mode):

  • Pet State: Pet name, chosen species (Kitten, Puppy, Bunny, Fox), coat color, unlocked 3D outfits, level, XP, coins, hunger, happiness, energy, and evolution stage.
  • Daily Habit Quests: Custom or preset habit titles, categories, streak counts, and completion timestamps.
  • Companion Chat & Playdates: Messages exchanged with your virtual pet, summarized pet memory notes, and friend codes or playdate logs.
  • Daily AI Quota Ledger: Daily and monthly usage counters for AI chat, photo verification, and sticker generation to enforce fair-use tier limits.

2.3 Camera & User-Selected Photos (Habit Proof Verification)

ChibiPet offers an optional Camera & Photo Proof feature that rewards bonus XP and Coins when you verify a completed habit with a photo (such as a glass of water, healthy meal, or book page):

  • Zero-Permission Photo Picker: When you pick an existing photo, the App uses the system Android Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia), which grants temporary read access only to the single image you explicitly select without requesting broad media storage permissions.
  • In-App Camera Viewfinder: When you capture a live photo, the App requests the runtime android.permission.CAMERA permission and captures a scaled image solely when you press the shutter button.
  • Transient AI Processing: Selected or captured habit proof photos are resized on-device and sent over an encrypted connection to Firebase AI Logic (Google Gemini Vision) solely to evaluate whether the image matches your habit category. Proof photos are processed transiently and are not permanently stored in cloud storage or used to train public third-party models.
  • Self-Report Alternative: Camera and photo access is completely optional; you can always complete habits using the built-in Self-Report Check-In option without providing a photo.

2.4 Generative AI Features (Pet Chat & Sticker Studio)

When you chat with your 3D pet or generate custom pet artwork in the AI Sticker & Poster Studio, your text prompt and pet context (name, species, mood, and active habits) are transmitted securely via Firebase AI Logic to generate a response or image. Generated stickers are saved locally to your device only when you choose to save them. When daily AI credits are exhausted, the App automatically falls back to on-device responses and local vector stickers without transmitting data to cloud AI endpoints.

3. Device Permissions Summary

Permission Purpose Required or Optional
android.permission.INTERNET Required to sync pet progress with Google Cloud Firestore, authenticate via Google Sign-In, and process cloud AI chat, habit verification, and sticker prompts. Install-time (Standard)
android.permission.CAMERA Used exclusively when you open the in-app CameraX viewfinder to snap a habit verification photo. Optional (Runtime Consent)
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used to deliver optional daily habit and pet care reminders scheduled locally via Android WorkManager. Optional (Runtime Consent)

You may revoke CAMERA or POST_NOTIFICATIONS permissions at any time in your device's Android System Settings without losing access to core habit tracking or 3D pet care features.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is processed only by trusted Google Cloud infrastructure providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Credential Manager: For secure Google Sign-In authentication.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: For encrypted cloud storage and real-time synchronization of your pet, habits, and quota counters, isolated by strict per-user security rules (request.auth.uid == userId).
  • Firebase App Check: To verify that requests originate from an authentic instance of the App and prevent abuse.
  • Firebase AI Logic (Google Gemini & Imagen): For conversational pet responses, transient habit photo verification, and custom pet sticker generation.

For more details on how Google processes data across Firebase services, please review the Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

  • Encryption in Transit and at Rest: All network traffic between the App and Firebase services is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS. Cloud Firestore data is encrypted at rest and protected by zero-trust security rules.
  • Local Preferences & Language Settings: Onboarding progress, preferred app language, local habit reminder times, and saved sticker files are stored locally in the App's private sandbox directory on your device.
  • Deleting Your Data:
    • Local Data: You can delete saved local stickers directly inside the Wardrobe & Studio screen, sign out from the Account panel, or clear all local app data via Android System Settings > Apps > ChibiPet > Storage > Clear Storage.
    • Cloud Account & Firestore Data: You may request complete deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and all associated Cloud Firestore documents (pet profile, habits, chat history, playdates, and quota records) at any time by contacting us at the support email below. Verified deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ChibiPet is designed as a general-audience habit and virtual pet companion. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal data without consent, please contact us immediately so we can delete the information.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Global Users)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of the personal data associated with your account.
  • Edit or update your pet profile, habits, and account preferences directly within the App.
  • Request deletion of your personal data and cloud account records.
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (Camera, Notifications, or Google Sign-In) at any time.
