Privacy Policy

FlagCraft: Flags & Geography • Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: FlagCraft is an independent, educational geography application. We collect no personal data, require no accounts, display zero third-party ads, use no telemetry trackers, and never sell or share user information.

1. Overview and Core Principles

FlagCraft ("we", "us", or "our") was created to provide a clean, distraction-free geography learning and living room trivia experience. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy describes how FlagCraft operates with respect to user information across Android handheld devices and Android TV systems.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

FlagCraft does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact lists
  • Precise or coarse GPS location data
  • Biometric data, camera imagery, or audio recordings
  • Device identifiers, Advertising IDs (AAID), or IMEI numbers
  • Browsing history, search queries, or analytics event streams

3. Local Device Storage

FlagCraft stores gameplay preferences strictly on your local device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Flashcard Mastery & Study Bookmarks: Your learned countries, study progression, and bookmarked regions.
  • High Scores & Quiz History: Blitz quiz and TV Party showdown results to display your personal statistics.
  • Non-Intrusive Engagement Logic: Installation timestamp and last-prompt timestamp used to enforce a 30-day cooldown between optional feedback inquiries.

All local data remains exclusively on your hardware and can be cleared at any time via Android App Settings > Storage > Clear Data.

4. Device Sensors (Accelerometer & Shake Gesture)

The application includes an optional shake detection feature to flip cards or advance rounds. Accelerometer sensor events are processed transiently in device memory (RAM) and are discarded immediately. Sensor data is never logged, stored, or transmitted over any network.

5. Network Requests & Open Geography API

FlagCraft is designed to work offline. For high-resolution country flag assets and geographical information, the app accesses public REST Countries API endpoints over secure HTTPS. These network calls transmit no user identity, account tokens, or telemetry.

6. In-App Purchases & Voluntary Developer Support

FlagCraft is completely functional and ad-free for all users. We offer voluntary developer tipping options:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processed directly and securely through the official Google Play Billing library. We never see or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.
  • External Voluntary Support (PayPal & Ko-fi): Tapping PayPal or Ko-fi links directs you to external web browsers governed independently by PayPal and Ko-fi privacy policies.

7. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

FlagCraft contains NO third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, or Meta Audience Network) and NO behavioral tracking trackers (such as Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, or Adjust).

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

FlagCraft complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. Because the app does not collect personal data from any user, it is safe for learners of all ages, including children under 13.

9. GDPR & CCPA / CPRA Compliance

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • We do not process personal data, so there is no data to sell, share, or profile.
  • Users have full control over their local data, which can be erased via device settings or by uninstalling the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update our privacy practices, the revised policy will be posted within this document and updated in our store listings. Continued use of the application following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
