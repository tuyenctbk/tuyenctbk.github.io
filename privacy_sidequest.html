1. Introduction

Welcome to SideQuest: Real-World Audio RPG. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe, transparent, and respectful manner. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We believe in the principle of minimal data collection. We only process information strictly required to provide the core gameplay and walking tour experience:

  • Location Data (Coarse & Fine Location): Used strictly on-device to detect nearby real-world landmarks, calculate walking route distances, and trigger location-based audio encounters. Your live GPS coordinates are never sold, rented, or tracked across external applications.
  • Authentication Information: We use Google Sign-In via Android Credential Manager to authenticate your adventurer profile securely. We store your public display name, email address, and unique user identifier in Google Cloud Firestore to sync your quest journal, earned badges, and relic inventory across your devices.
  • Camera & Photo Access: Used exclusively when you choose to scan historical plaques or landmark monuments via Gemini Multimodal Vision. Images selected via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker are processed to generate in-character lore and are not retained on external servers beyond the immediate verification request.
  • Microphone & Speech Data: Used only when you initiate voice notes or spoken dialogue with the Gemini Dungeon Master. Audio is processed for on-device/real-time transcription and is not stored or shared for advertising purposes.

3. Cloud Services & Third-Party Processors

SideQuest partners with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers that adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure sign-in and encrypted cloud persistence of your quest journal, badges, and adventurer stats.
  • Firebase AI Logic (Gemini API): For generating contextual in-character walking quests, historical fact dossiers, and cipher riddles without passing your personal profile to advertising networks.
  • OpenStreetMap: For public point-of-interest cartographic data.

4. Advertising and Monetization Policy

SideQuest is 100% Ad-Free. We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or user-tracking analytics trackers. All voluntary support options (such as PayPal, Ko-fi, and Google Play Supporter Passes) are strictly user-initiated, static in Settings, and never presented through intrusive pop-ups.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any completed quest log or custom artifact directly inside the Quest Journal and Codex Vault at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your adventurer profile and associated Firestore documents by signing out or contacting our support team. Upon confirmation, all associated data is permanently erased from our databases within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

SideQuest does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL) for all data transmitted between your device and Google Cloud servers. Data stored in Cloud Firestore is protected by zero-trust security rules ensuring only authenticated users can access their own journals and inventories.
