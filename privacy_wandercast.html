WanderCast ("we", "our", or "the App") is an AI-powered GPS walking tour and neighborhood radio documentary application. We design WanderCast with a local-first, privacy-by-design architecture where your walk history, audio documentary archive, unlocked digital badges, and app event logs remain stored locally on your device in an on-device SQLite (Room) database.

2. Location Data & Foreground Walk Service

  • Real-Time Geofence Detection: WanderCast uses your device's GPS coordinates while the app is open (or while you explicitly enable the Foreground Walk Service notification) to determine your current street block and proximity to historical checkpoints.
  • Visible Foreground Notification: When background walk tracking is toggled on, WanderCast displays a persistent notification ("99.1 FM • WanderCast Walking Guide") with an instant "Stop Walk" action so you remain in full control at all times.
  • Adaptive Battery Protection: When your device battery falls below 20%, WanderCast automatically switches to Balanced Power GPS polling (18-second intervals, 28-meter displacement) and throttles map tile refreshes to conserve battery life.
  • No Sale or Advertising Tracking: We never sell, rent, or share your GPS coordinates with advertising networks or data brokers.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Services

To synthesize localized neighborhood documentaries, walking routes, and map tiles, WanderCast interacts with the following services only when needed:

  • Google Firebase AI Logic & Gemini Models: Street block names, approximate coordinates, and user-submitted questions or landmark photos are sent over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) to generate documentary scripts, trivia questions, and architectural analysis. App integrity is verified via Firebase App Check.
  • Android On-Device Text-to-Speech (TTS): Voice narration is synthesized locally on your device using Android's built-in TextToSpeech engine (0 cloud audio streaming cost and 0 voice recording collection).
  • Interactive Map Tiles: The Tour Summary route preview loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, CartoDB, or Esri over HTTPS, or renders 100% offline using the built-in Vector Topographic Radar canvas.

4. Data Retention, User Control & Deletion

  • 100% On-Device Control: All recorded episodes, walk routes, unlocked digital badges, and app event logs are stored locally on your device.
  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual episodes or completed walk routes in the Archive (Field Tapes) tab, clear logged app events in the Rewards & Telemetry tab, or remove your optional BYOK key at any time.
  • Complete Erasure: Uninstalling WanderCast or clearing the app's storage in Android Settings permanently erases all local Room database tables and preferences.

5. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

WanderCast is a general-audience urban history and walking guide application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. The updated effective date will always be displayed at the top of this page.
