Privacy Summary: PantryPulse is built with an Offline-First, 100% Ad-Free, Privacy-By-Design architecture. Your fridge inventory, expiration countdowns, Smart Shopping List, Shelf-Life Preservation Coach plans, kitchen appliance profile, local engagement telemetry, and saved rescue recipes are stored locally on your Android device in an encrypted-capable SQLite (Room) database and SharedPreferences. We do not sell personal data, display third-party behavioral advertising, or track your location.

2. Android Permissions & Why We Request Them

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA — Optional Runtime Permission): Used exclusively inside the Fridge & Receipt Scanner viewfinder so you can photograph open fridge shelves or grocery receipts. If you decline Camera permission, you can still use the zero-permission Android Photo Picker, Voice-to-Inventory dictation, or Manual Ingredient Input without restriction.
  • Internet & Network State (android.permission.INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to communicate securely over HTTPS/TLS with Firebase AI Logic (Gemini API) when Cloud Tier 1 (Standard) or Tier 2 (Eco Lite) is active.
  • Zero Broad Media Permissions: In compliance with Google Play Photo & Video Permissions policy, PantryPulse never requests READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO. Gallery selection uses the system Android Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia).

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing & Zero-Cloud Local Mode

PantryPulse provides a transparent 3-Tier AI capability architecture:

  • Tier 1 (Standard Cloud AI) & Tier 2 (Eco Lite Cloud AI): When online and within your daily free-tier allowance, fridge photos (downscaled to ≤896px), pantry ingredient names, shelf-life preservation queries, and selected kitchen appliances are sent over encrypted TLS to Google Firebase AI Logic (Gemini models) to detect ingredients, estimate expiration days, recommend zero-waste preservation methods, and generate ranked rescue recipes.
  • Tier 3 (100% On-Device Local Culinary Engine): You can enable Local Tier 3 Mode at any time from the AI Value Receipt & Quota Guardrails dialog (or automatically when offline). In Local Tier 3 Mode, all recipe synthesis, shelf-life preservation tips, spoken grocery parsing, visual chef plating cards, and Sous-Chef Q&A execute deterministically on your device with zero cloud transmissions.

4. Data Retention, User Control & Deletion

  • Full On-Device Control: You can edit, mark as consumed ("Used Up"), restock via the Smart Shopping List, clear local event logs, or permanently delete any ingredient or recipe at any time directly inside the PantryPulse app.
  • Instant Complete Wipe: Clearing the application storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → PantryPulse → Storage & cache → Clear storage) or uninstalling PantryPulse immediately and permanently erases all local Room database records, cached recipes, and preferences.

5. Children's Privacy

PantryPulse is a household utility and meal-planning application intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Security Practices

All network requests to Firebase AI Logic use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Local data resides inside Android's sandboxed application storage directory protected by OS-level file permissions.
