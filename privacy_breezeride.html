Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Welcome to BreezeRide. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information in connection with the BreezeRide Android application.

1. Information We Collect

BreezeRide operates primarily as a client-side assistant to ensure total privacy and physical security. We access or collect the following categories of data:

  • Location & Telemetry Data: Real-time GPS location and speed to support active pacing, speedometer HUD display, and route-mapping. This data is processed in real-time on your device and is never uploaded to any external server.
  • Sensors & Incident Detection: Accelerometer and gyroscope telemetry are analyzed solely on-device to detect sudden impacts or potential crashes for safety SOS alerting.
  • Guardian Emergency Contacts: Standard phone number and contact details input for the SOS Guardian are stored locally in the secure room database on your device and are only accessed when triggering automated safe emergency alerts.
  • Maintenance Logs: Bike mileage and components usage thresholds are tracked offline via local SQLite/Room database structures.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected details strictly to provide core features of the app:

  • Real-time telemetry and speed coaching.
  • Sending SMS notifications or placing emergency SOS alerts to your specified guardian in the event of an incident.
  • Tracking component mileage thresholds to warn you before safety limits are reached.
  • Displaying carbon footprint and health analytics locally on your dashboard.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Your privacy is absolute. BreezeRide does not sell, rent, or distribute any location, sensor, or contact details to third-party brokers. We may include Google AdMob services to serve light-weight banner advertisements. Any data collected by ad networks is governed strictly by Google's Privacy Policies.

4. Data Retention and Security

All records—including ride logs, guardian contact details, offline map regions, and component thresholds—are kept strictly within your private device database. Deleting or clearing the BreezeRide application data will permanently remove all stored local databases.

5. Your Rights and Permissions

You can grant, deny, or revoke any Android permission (such as Background Location, SMS, Contacts) at any time through your Android Settings menu. Denying background location may restrict the ability of the HUD and SOS engines to calculate active pacing and accident telemetry.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by publishing the updated policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at: support@breezeride.aistudio.com
