Privacy Policy for SoundSlumber

Effective Date: August 13, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SoundSlumber ("we," "our," or "us"). We value your privacy and are dedicated to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how SoundSlumber operates, what data is processed locally on your device, and your rights regarding your information.

By using SoundSlumber, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Used

SoundSlumber is built with a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture. Data generated during your usage remains stored locally on your device.

A. Local Storage & Playback Data (Room Database)

  • Listening History & Usage Metrics: SoundSlumber tracks listening duration, session counts, and played audio tracks locally using an on-device Room database. This data is used exclusively to populate your personal Sleep Sound Dashboard, provide insights, and calculate smart suggestions (such as update recommendations based on milestone usage).
  • Mix Presets & Audio Settings: Custom sound combinations, individual track volume levels, master volume caps, and background visual associations are stored locally on your device.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as dark theme schedules and custom sleep timer presets are saved on-device.

B. Device Permissions & System Features

  • Audio & Foreground Service: Required for continuous ambient sound playback, background execution during sleep sessions, and volume attenuation (fade-out sleep timer).
  • System Time & Clock: Used locally to sync low-light display modes (such as Night Amber Mode) with system sunset and sunrise times to reduce blue light exposure.

3. Data Transmission & Third-Party Sharing

  • No Selling of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetarily exchange your personal data or listening history with third parties or advertising brokers.
  • On-Device Storage: SoundSlumber does not require account registration or transmit your private listening history to external cloud database servers.
  • Google Play Services: If distributed via the Google Play Store, standard platform SDKs (such as Google Play Services) may process basic device diagnostics in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. AI-Generated Content & Disclosures

SoundSlumber utilizes generative AI capabilities (such as Imagen) to create calming background visuals for saved Mix Presets. All generated visual assets are rendered strictly for personal in-app visual identity and do not harvest or send user personal data.

5. Data Control, Retention & Deletion

Because your dashboard history, listening logs, and saved presets reside entirely in your local app storage:

  • In-App Management: You can modify, overwrite, or delete saved Mix Presets and clear session logs within the app interface.
  • Data Erasure: You can completely erase all app data at any time by navigating to your device's settings (Settings > Apps > SoundSlumber > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children’s Privacy

SoundSlumber is a general-audience ambient audio companion. It does not knowingly collect personal identifying information from children under 13 years of age, nor does it require online account creation.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to align with new features or regulatory requirements (including Google Play Developer Policies). Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
