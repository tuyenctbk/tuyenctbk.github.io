Privacy Policy

ArtGallery TV

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Privacy First Commitment: ArtGallery TV is built to bring world-class museum masterpieces to living rooms and desk stands. We do not track, collect, sell, or transmit any personal user data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy. ArtGallery TV does not collect, record, or share:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account registrations.
  • No Precise Location Data: We do not track or access GPS or fine network location.
  • No Sensor Telemetry: Accelerometer data used for optional shake gestures is processed locally in temporary device memory and is never logged or transmitted.
  • No User Profiling or Behavioral Tracking: There are no third-party analytics trackers, behavioral cookies, or advertising SDKs.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth experience across launches, certain non-sensitive operational preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore / Room Database:

  • Your chosen slideshow transition speed and placard display preferences.
  • Artworks marked as favorites in your Private Gallery.
  • Selected department filters and soundscape choices.
  • Local usage counts (such as slide view count) used to trigger optional milestone appreciation.

This data stays on your local device storage and is never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

ArtGallery TV connects to network services solely to fetch public-domain museum content and process optional voluntary support:

  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access API: Artwork metadata and images are retrieved directly from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access public collection (Creative Commons Zero / CC0).
  • Google Play In-App Billing: If you voluntarily choose to support ArtGallery TV via in-app patron tiers, transactions are handled securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or financial details.
  • Optional Voluntary Links (PayPal / Ko-fi): If you choose to tip via PayPal or Ko-fi, these open external browser links governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Children\'s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ArtGallery TV does not target children under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children. All featured artworks are cultural museum masterworks in the public domain.

5. Google Play Developer Policy Compliance

ArtGallery TV strictly complies with Google Play Developer Program policies, including the User Data policy, Family policy, and advertising guidelines. The app contains zero third-party advertisements.

6. Permissions Used

The app requests only standard install-time permissions necessary for displaying museum artwork:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch public-domain art images from museum repositories.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check connectivity and seamlessly fallback to bundled curated offline masterworks when offline.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
